## Purpose

This template structures the annual IT budget planning process. It is designed to capture all cost categories, surface the rationale behind each line item, and support the approval conversation with finance and leadership.

A budget that cannot be explained is a budget that gets cut. Every line here should have a clear owner and a clear justification.
