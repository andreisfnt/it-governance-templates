## Purpose

This framework provides a structured, repeatable process for evaluating technology vendors before procurement decisions. It is designed to surface risk early, enable comparison across vendors, and create an auditable record of the selection rationale.

Use this document for new vendor relationships. For renewals, use it to reassess vendors where scope, risk level, or business criticality has changed.

## Evaluation Criteria and Scoring

Score each vendor from 1 to 5 for each criterion. Weight reflects business priority and may be adjusted per evaluation.

**Scoring guide:** 1 = Does not meet requirements, 2 = Partially meets, 3 = Meets requirements, 4 = Exceeds, 5 = Significantly exceeds
