# IT Governance Templates

A collection of reusable IT governance documents for IT leaders, operations teams, and IT managers. These templates are generic by design - adaptable to organizations of any size, sector, or maturity level.

Replace placeholder text in `[brackets]` with your organization's specifics. All templates are vendor-neutral and technology-agnostic.

## Principles behind these templates

**Completeness over brevity.** A template that skips the hard parts isn't useful. These are designed to surface the questions that matter, not just the easy ones.

**Process, not tooling.** These documents describe what good looks like. How you implement it depends on your environment.

**Usable from day one.** Each template is self-contained. No dependencies, no prerequisites beyond a text editor.
